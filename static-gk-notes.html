Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Which is the only floating national park in the world and in which Indian state is it situated?
Options: (A) Sundarbans, West Bengal  |  (B) Keibul Lamjao, Manipur  |  (C) Simlipal, Odisha  |  (D) Silent Valley, Kerala
Factual Breakdown The park is characterized by floating decomposed plant matter known locally as phumdis on Loktak Lake.
Species Clue It is the exclusive natural home of the endangered Sangai (brow-antlered deer).
Solution Correct park is Keibul Lamjao National Park, Manipur (Option B).
✓ Final Answer: Keibul Lamjao, Manipur
Ex 2 Match the Indian river dam with its corresponding river and state:
1. Tehri Dam  |  2. Hirakud Dam  |  3. Sardar Sarovar Dam  |  4. Nagarjuna Sagar Dam

• Hirakud Dam: Mahanadi River (Odisha)

• Nagarjuna Sagar Dam: Krishna River (Andhra Pradesh / Telangana)

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing Kathak (UP) with Kathakali (Kerala)
Kathak is the classical dance of North India / Uttar Pradesh (associated with Pandit Birju Maharaj). Kathakali is the classical dance-drama of Kerala featuring elaborate face masks and makeup.
🚫
Mistake 2: Reversing Highest vs Longest River Dams
Tehri Dam (260.5 m) is India's Highest dam. Hirakud Dam (total length 25.79 km) is India's Longest earthen dam.
🚫
Mistake 3: Confusing Ramsar Site Origins
The Ramsar Convention on Wetlands was signed in Ramsar, Iran in 1971 (effective 1975). Chilika Lake (Odisha) and Keoladeo National Park (Rajasthan) were the first Indian sites designated in 1981.
