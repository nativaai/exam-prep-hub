Master 2D plane geometry (Areas & Perimeters) and 3D solid geometry (Surface Areas, Volumes & Melting Conversions).

💡

2D vs 3D Mensuration Fundamentals

2D Figures (Plane Geometry): Bounded by length and breadth. Measured in Perimeter (linear boundary, units: cm/m) and Area (surface enclosed, units: cm²/m²).

3D Figures (Solid Geometry): Bounded by length, breadth, and height. Measured in Curved/Total Surface Area and Volume (capacity enclosed, units: cm³/m³).

Core Invariant Rule (Melting/Recasting):
• When a 3D solid is melted and recast into another shape, Total Volume Remains Constant.
📐

Essential 2D & 3D Formulas

⭕ Circle & Semi-Circle
Area = πr² | Peri = 2πr
Semi-circle Perimeter = πr + 2r = r(36/7).
🔺 Triangle & Right Triangle
Area = ½ × b × h = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]
Equilateral Triangle Area = (√3 / 4)a² | Height = (√3 / 2)a.
🥫 Cylinder & Cone
Cylinder Vol = πr²h | Cone Vol = ⅓πr²h
Cone Slant Height l = √(r² + h²) | CSA = πrl.
⚽ Sphere & Hemisphere
Sphere Vol = ⁴⁄₃πr³ | TSA = 4πr²
Hemisphere CSA = 2πr² | TSA = 3πr² | Vol = ⅔πr³.
📝

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Find the area of a circle whose circumference is 88 cm. (Use π = 22/7)
Given Circumference (2πr) = 88 cm
Radius Formula 2 × (22 / 7) × r = 88 → r = (88 × 7) / 44 = 14 cm
Area Calculation Area = πr² = (22 / 7) × 14 × 14 = 22 × 2 × 14 = 616 cm²
✓ Final Answer: Area = 616 cm²
Ex 2 A solid metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into smaller spheres of radius 2 cm each. Find the number of smaller spheres formed.
Given Big Sphere Radius (R) = 6 cm, Small Sphere Radius (r) = 2 cm
Volume Ratio Rule Number of Spheres (N) = Volume_big / Volume_small = (⁴⁄₃πR³) / (⁴⁄₃πr³) = (R / r)³
Solution N = (6 / 2)³ = (3)³ = 27 spheres
✓ Final Answer: 27 Spheres
Ex 3 Find the length of the longest rod that can be placed inside a rectangular room of dimensions 12 m × 9 m × 8 m.
Given l = 12 m, b = 9 m, h = 8 m
Body Diagonal Longest Rod (Diagonal) = √(l² + b² + h²)
Solution √(12² + 9² + 8²) = √(144 + 81 + 64) = √289 = 17 m
✓ Final Answer: 17 meters

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

✨
Divisibility by 11 (π Check): In Mensuration problems involving π (22/7), the answer options are almost always divisible by 11. Test digit differences to eliminate options fast!
📐
Pythagorean Triplets: Memorize standard right-triangle triplets: (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (7,24,25), (8,15,17), (9,40,41), (20,21,29).
🔄
Radius Multipliers: If radius is multiplied by k → Circumference increases by k, Area increases by k², Volume increases by k³.
🛣️
Running Track / Pathway Area: Area of path width w outside a rectangle (l×b) = 2w(l + b + 2w); inside = 2w(l + b − 2w).
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Forgetting the Diameter in Semi-Circle Perimeter
Perimeter of a semi-circle is πr + 2r (boundary includes the straight base diameter), NOT simply πr.
🚫
Mistake 2: Confusing CSA and TSA of a Hemisphere
Curved Surface Area is 2πr², while Total Surface Area (including flat circular base) is 3πr².
🚫
Mistake 3: Mixing Height (h) and Slant Height (l) in Cones
Volume requires vertical height h [V = ⅓πr²h], whereas Curved Surface Area requires slant height l [CSA = πrl = πr√(r² + h²)].
