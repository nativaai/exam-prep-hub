⏱️ Time & Work - Study Notes

Master the Unit-Work / LCM method, individual & group efficiencies, MDH formula, and alternate-day work patterns.

🛠️
💡

Concept & Definition

Work: Considered as 1 complete unit (or total units derived using the LCM of given days).

Efficiency: The amount of work done by a person in a single unit of time (1 day or 1 hour).

Core Inverse Relationship:
• Time and Efficiency are inversely proportional.
• If A takes n days to finish a task, A's 1-day work = 1 / n
• Total Work = Efficiency × Time Taken
📐

Core Time & Work Formulas

🔹 Combined Time (A & B)
Time = (x × y) / (x + y)
When A alone takes x days and B alone takes y days.
⚙️ The MDH Formula
(M₁ × D₁ × H₁) / W₁ = (M₂ × D₂ × H₂) / W₂
M = Men, D = Days, H = Hours/day, W = Work units.
⚡ Three Persons Combined
Time = xyz / (xy + yz + zx)
When A, B, and C take x, y, and z days respectively.
💰 Wages Distribution
Wages ∝ Total Work Done
If time is same, wages divide in ratio of efficiencies.
📝

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 A can complete a task in 12 days and B can complete it in 18 days. Working together, how long will they take?
Given A = 12 days, B = 18 days → Assume Total Work = LCM(12, 18) = 36 units
Efficiencies A's efficiency = 36 / 12 = 3 units/day  |  B's efficiency = 36 / 18 = 2 units/day
Solution Combined Efficiency = 3 + 2 = 5 units/day → Time = 36 / 5 = 7.2 days (7 ⅕ days)
Total Units 36 Units
÷
Rate (A + B) 5 Units / Day
=
Days Needed 7.2 Days
✓ Final Answer: 7.2 Days
Ex 2 15 men working 8 hours a day can build a wall in 10 days. In how many days can 20 men working 6 hours a day build the same wall?
Given M₁ = 15, H₁ = 8, D₁ = 10, W₁ = 1  |  M₂ = 20, H₂ = 6, W₂ = 1, D₂ = ?
Formula (M₁ × D₁ × H₁) = (M₂ × D₂ × H₂)
Solution 15 × 10 × 8 = 20 × D₂ × 6 → 1200 = 120 × D₂ → D₂ = 1200 / 120 = 10 days
✓ Final Answer: 10 Days
Ex 3 A is twice as efficient as B. If together they finish a work in 14 days, in how many days can A alone complete it?
Given Efficiency Ratio (A : B) = 2 : 1 → Combined Efficiency = 2 + 1 = 3 units/day
Calculation Total Work = (Combined Efficiency) × (Time) = 3 × 14 = 42 units
Solution Time for A alone = Total Work / A's Efficiency = 42 / 2 = 21 days
✓ Final Answer: 21 Days

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

🎯
🎯
LCM Method: Always assume Total Work = LCM of individual day counts to work with clean integers instead of fractions.
⚡
Leaving Before Completion: If a person leaves x days before scheduled completion, add their x days of hypothetical work to the Total Work.
🔄
Alternate Days Pattern: Calculate work done in 1 full cycle (e.g., 2 days for A then B), divide total work by cycle work, then complete remaining units sequentially.
🚰
Pipes & Cisterns Analogy: Inlet pipes represent positive efficiency (+), while drainage/leak pipes represent negative efficiency (−).
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Directly Adding Days Taken
Never add days directly (e.g., A in 10 days + B in 15 days does not equal 25 days). Always add daily efficiencies: 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 → 6 days.
🚫
Mistake 2: Splitting Wages by Days When Efficiencies Differ
Wages must be divided based on actual units of work delivered by each person, not simply their presence on the job.
🚫
Mistake 3: Misplacing Work in MDH Formula
Ensure Work (W) is in the denominator: (M₁ D₁ H₁) / W₁ = (M₂ D₂ H₂) / W₂. Number of toys made, meters dug, or walls built are Work quantities.
