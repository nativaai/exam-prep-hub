Teaching Concept, Triadic Model & Objectives

Teaching is a purposeful, interpersonal interactive process designed to bring desirable behavioural changes in the learner.

The Triadic Nature (John Dewey):
• Independent Variable: Teacher (Plans, guides, directs).
• Dependent Variable: Learner (Active participant subjected to changes).
• Intervening Variables: Curriculum, instructional methods, environment, support systems.
📐

Levels of Teaching & Teacher Roles

🧠 Memory Level (MLT)
Propounded by Herbart
Thoughtless teaching focused on rote learning, recall, recognition of facts, and stimulus-response conditioning. Primary emphasis for lower classes.
💡 Understanding Level (ULT)
Propounded by Morrison
Thoughtful teaching focused on comprehension, grasping relationships, principles, generalizing facts, and identifying similarities/differences.
🔍 Reflective Level (RLT)
Propounded by Hunt
Highly thoughtful, problem-centric teaching. Fosters critical inquiry, creative analysis, independent thinking, and hypothesis testing. Learner-dominated.
🎯 Bloom's Taxonomy Domains
Cognitive | Affective | Psychomotor
Cognitive: Knowledge, Understanding, Application, Analysis, Synthesis, Evaluation. Affective: Emotions & Values. Psychomotor: Motor skills.
⚖️

Teaching Methods & Support Infrastructure

👨‍🏫 Teacher-Centered (Pedagogy)
Lecture | Demonstration | Direct Instruction
Teacher acts as primary information dispenser. Learners remain passive recipients. Authoritarian or paternalistic classroom control.
🧑‍🎓 Learner-Centered (Andragogy)
Flipped Class | Heuristic | Project | Seminar
Learner is autonomous and actively constructs knowledge. Teacher acts as a facilitator or guide. Promotes inquiry and self-paced learning.
💻 Digital & ICT Support Systems
SWAYAM | SWAYAM PRABHA | MOOCs
SWAYAM: 4 quadrants (Video, Text, Self-assessment, Discussion). SWAYAM PRABHA: DTH educational TV channels via GSAT-15.
📊 Evaluation & Grading Models
Formative | Summative | CBCS System
Formative: Continuous assessment *during* instruction. Summative: Terminal judgement *at the end*. CBCS: Choice Based Credit System.

Step-by-Step Solved UGC NET Examples

Ex 1 A teacher conducts diagnostic quizzes, poses probing questions, and provides immediate descriptive feedback during everyday classroom instruction. Which type of evaluation is being conducted?
Options: (A) Summative  |  (B) Formative  |  (C) Norm-Referenced  |  (D) Criterion-Referenced
Analysis Evaluation conducted during the instructional process to monitor learning progress and provide continuous feedback to both teacher and students.
Rule Formative = Ongoing / Diagnostic / Improvement-oriented. Summative = Terminal / Grading / Certification.
Solution Since the quizzes and feedback are happening during active teaching, it is Formative Evaluation (Option B).
✓ Correct Answer: Formative Evaluation
Ex 2 Match the Levels of Teaching with their respective primary exponents:
1. Memory Level  |  2. Understanding Level  |  3. Reflective Level
Theorist Mapping • Memory Level: Herbart (Rote memorization, recall, stimulus response).
• Understanding Level: Morrison (Concept mastery, relations, generalization).
• Reflective Level: Hunt (Problem-solving, critical analysis, creative synthesis).
✓ Exponents: Herbart (MLT) → Morrison (ULT) → Hunt (RLT)

Speed-Solving Mnemonics & Exam Shortcuts

Teaching Levels Mnemonic:
Memory → Understanding → Reflective (M-U-R)
Remember exponents via H-M-H (Herbart, Morrison, Hunt).

CRT vs NRT Evaluation:
• Criterion-Referenced (CRT): Evaluates against a fixed standard/mastery (e.g. scoring >75%).
• Norm-Referenced (NRT): Ranks students relative to peers (Percentiles, Rank 1).
📺
SWAYAM 4 Quadrants:
1. e-Tutorial (Video lectures)
2. e-Content (Downloadable text / Reading)
3. Assessment (Quizzes & Tests)
4. Discussion Forum (Doubt clearing).
🎯
Gagné's 9 Events of Instruction:
Gain attention → Inform objectives → Stimulate recall → Present stimulus → Provide guidance → Elicit performance → Feedback → Assess → Enhance retention.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing Formative vs Summative Evaluation
Formative occurs during instruction to improve teaching-learning processes (diagnostic quizzes, homework). Summative occurs at the end of a term/course for grading, ranking, and final certification.
🚫
Mistake 2: Mixing Up Pedagogy, Andragogy, and Heutagogy
Pedagogy is teacher-led learning (child-centric/dependent). Andragogy is adult self-directed learning. Heutagogy is completely self-determined and self-directed learning where the learner decides what and how to learn.
🚫
Mistake 3: Misinterpreting CBCS Grading Components
Under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), courses are divided into Core Courses (compulsory), Elective Courses (pools of specialization choices), and Ability Enhancement Courses (foundation/skill-based). It uses a 10-point grading scale.
