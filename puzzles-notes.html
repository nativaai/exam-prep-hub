Matrix Construction Framework

Analytical Puzzles require organizing scattered parameters (e.g. Persons, Floors, Professions, Months, Colors) into an unambiguous relational grid.

Primary Rule for Base Selection:
• Always fix the most invariant variable as your grid's fixed spine:
  • Floors: Bottom (1) to Top (N)
  • Dates/Months: Chronological sequence (Jan → Dec)
  • Days: Monday → Sunday
📐

Core Puzzle Archetypes

🏢 Floor & Flat Based
Top = High | Ground = 1
Flat A is West of Flat B on Floor k. Fix floors vertically first.
📦 Box Stacking / Ordering
Relative Gaps: "k Boxes Between"
Positions are not pre-numbered; combine relative blocks first.
📅 Scheduling (Days/Months/Years)
Chronological Fixing
Note days in month (30 vs 31) and leap year constraints immediately.
📊 Multi-Variable Tabulation
Cross (Tick / Cross) Grid
Used when 3+ independent attributes (Name, City, Car) are assigned.

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 5 people (A, B, C, D, E) live on a 5-floor building (Ground=1, Top=5). E lives on an odd floor above Floor 2. Only two persons live between E and A. B lives immediately below D. Who lives on Floor 1?
Step 1 (E's cases) E lives on odd floor > 2 → E can be on Floor 3 or Floor 5.
Step 2 (A's placement) If E = Floor 5 → 2 floors gap → A = Floor 2.
If E = Floor 3 → 2 floors gap → Not possible (below would need Floor 0, above needs Floor 6). Thus, E = Floor 5, A = Floor 2.
Step 3 (B & D block) B lives immediately below D → Pair [D, B] must fit in remaining consecutive empty slots (Floors 4, 3). So D = 4, B = 3. Remaining person C = Floor 1.
✓ Final Answer: C lives on Floor 1
Ex 2 4 boxes P, Q, R, S are stacked one above another. Box R is above Box S. Two boxes are kept between Box P and Box S. Box Q is kept at the bottom-most position. What is the order from top to bottom?
Anchor Bottom-most (Position 1) = Q.
Gap Clue Remaining positions are 4, 3, 2. Two boxes between P and S → P and S must occupy Position 4 and Position 2 (gap of 2: Pos 3 & Pos 2). Since R is between them, S cannot be at 1 (occupied by Q). Thus P = 4, S = 1 is invalid, so P = 4, S = 2 (or vice versa).
Ordering Since R is above S → R = 3, S = 2, P = 4 → Stack order (4 → 1): P, R, S, Q.
✓ Final Answer: P - R - S - Q (Top to Bottom)

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

✨
Negative Information Margin: Write negative constraints (e.g. A ≠ Floor 3) directly beside the relevant row to prevent illegal placements.
⚡
Chunking Relative Clues: Group linked clues together in short notation (e.g. [A __ B] for 1 person gap) before inserting into the main table.
📅
Month Day Tags: In month puzzles, write the total days (30 vs 31) next to each month name immediately to handle "born in a month with 30 days" clues.
🔀
Dual Case Rule: Never spend time hesitating between two valid cases. Draw Case 1 and Case 2 simultaneously; the false one will eliminate itself within 2-3 clues.
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing "Floors Above" with "Floors Gap"
"A lives 3 floors above B" means Floor(A) = Floor(B) + 3 (which is a gap of 2 floors between them, not 3).
🚫
Mistake 2: Starting with Variable Data instead of the Constant Base
Never fix persons as column headers when days, months, or floor numbers are available. Fixed sequential parameters must always form the base framework.
🚫
Mistake 3: Overlooking "Immediately Above / Below" vs "Above / Below"
"A lives above B" means anywhere above B on any higher floor, whereas "A lives immediately above B" restricts A to the exact adjacent upper floor (gap = 0).
