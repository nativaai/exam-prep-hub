Geographical Location & Boundaries

India is situated entirely in the Northern and Eastern Hemispheres, covering an area of 3.28 million sq. km (7th largest country in the world, 2.4% of world land area).

Spatial Coordinates & Extent:
• Latitudinal Extent: $8^\circ 4'\text{ N}$ to $37^\circ 6'\text{ N}$ (Mainland)
• Longitudinal Extent: $68^\circ 7'\text{ E}$ to $97^\circ 25'\text{ E}$
• Standard Meridian: $82^\circ 30'\text{ E}$ (Passes near Mirzapur/Prayagraj, UTC+5:30)
• Total Coastline: 7,516.6 km (Mainland: 6,100 km)

Major Geographical Systems

⛰️ Mountain Ranges & Peaks
Himalayas | Western Ghats
Highest Peak in India: Kangchenjunga (8,586m). Highest in S. India: Anamudi (2,695m). Oldest fold mountains: Aravalli.
🌊 River Drainage Systems
Himalayan vs Peninsular
Himalayan (Perennial): Ganga, Indus, Brahmaputra. Peninsular: Godavari (Dakshin Ganga), Narmada & Tapti (Estuaries/West-flowing).
🌦️ Climate & Monsoon Flow
SW Monsoon | NE Monsoon
SW Monsoon (June-Sept) brings 80% rain. NE Retreating Monsoon brings winter rain to Coromandel Coast (Tamil Nadu).
🌱 Soil Types & Distribution
Alluvial | Black (Regur)
Alluvial (40%, Northern plains). Black Soil (Deccan trap, best for Cotton). Laterite (High leaching, Tea/Coffee/Cashew).

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Which of the following Indian states does NOT lie along the Tropic of Cancer ($23^\circ 30'\text{ N}$)?
Options: (A) Gujarat  |  (B) Odisha  |  (C) Chhattisgarh  |  (D) Mizoram
Geographical Rule The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian states from West to East.
State Sequence 1. Gujarat  |  2. Rajasthan  |  3. Madhya Pradesh  |  4. Chhattisgarh  |  5. Jharkhand  |  6. West Bengal  |  7. Tripura  |  8. Mizoram.
Solution Odisha (Option B) lies south of the Tropic of Cancer and is not crossed by it.
✓ Final Answer: Odisha
Ex 2 Match the Peninsular River with its distinctive feature:
A. Narmada  |  B. Godavari  |  C. Kaveri (Cauvery)
River Characteristics • Narmada: Flows west through a rift valley between Vindhya and Satpura ranges; forms Marble Rocks at Jabalpur & Estuary at Gulf of Khambhat.
• Godavari: Longest peninsular river (1,465 km), known as Dakshin Ganga or Vridha Ganga.
• Kaveri: Receives rainfall from both SW Monsoon (summer) and NE Retreating Monsoon (winter), making it a near-perennial peninsular river.
✓ Peninsular River Features Verified

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing "Dakshin Ganga" with "Ganga of the South"
Godavari is officially referred to as Dakshin Ganga (due to length and age), whereas Kaveri (Cauvery) is often called Ganga of the South (due to religious sacredness and perennial nature).
🚫
Mistake 2: Assuming West-Flowing Rivers Form Deltas
West-flowing rivers like Narmada and Tapti flow rapidly through hard rock rift valleys and drop directly into the Arabian Sea, forming Estuaries instead of Deltas.
🚫
Mistake 3: Misidentifying the Highest Peak in India
K2 (Godwin-Austen) is the highest peak located in Indian territory (PoK). Kangchenjunga (Sikkim) is the highest peak in undisputed Indian territory. Anamudi is the highest in South India (Western Ghats).
