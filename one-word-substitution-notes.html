Thematic Root Grouping Framework

One Word Substitution (OWS) tests your ability to condense a lengthy phrase into a single, precise lexical term.

Categorical Suffix Keys:
• -cracy / -archy: Rule or Government (e.g. Democracy, Anarchy)
• -cide: Killing or Murder (e.g. Homicide, Regicide)
• -phobia: Morbid Fear (e.g. Claustrophobia)
• -ist: A person following a belief or profession (e.g. Altruist)
📐

High-Frequency Word Clusters

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Substitute the phrase with one word: "A person who present on all occasions and present everywhere at the same time"
Options: (A) Omniscient  |  (B) Omnipotent  |  (C) Omnipresent  |  (D) Optimist
Root Breakdown Prefix Omni- = All / Everywhere.
Option Decoder • Omniscient: All-knowing (sci = know)
• Omnipotent: All-powerful (potent = power)
• Omnipresent: Present everywhere (present = location)
Solution Correct term is Omnipresent (Option C).
✓ Final Answer: Omnipresent
Ex 2 Substitute the phrase: "One who is difficult to please or satisfy"
Options: (A) Fastidious  |  (B) Gullible  |  (C) Infallible  |  (D) Fanatic
Definitions • Gullible: Easily deceived.
• Infallible: Incapable of making mistakes.
• Fastidious: Excessively hard to please / meticulous to a fault.
Solution Correct word is Fastidious (Option A).
✓ Final Answer: Fastidious

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

✨
Suffix Identification: Use suffixes to immediately identify categories (e.g. -logy = Study of, -graphy = Writing style, -vorous = Feeding on).
🎯
Phil vs Mis: Phil- denotes Love (e.g. Philogynist = lover of women); Mis- denotes Hatred (e.g. Misogynist = hater of women).
⚡
Theistic Root (Theo): Theist = believes in God; Atheist = denies God's existence; Agnostic = doubts God's existence.
🔍
Eliminate Opposite Polarities: When a phrase describes a positive virtue (e.g. "one who helps the poor"), eliminate terms containing negative roots (e.g. mis- or mal-).
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing Atheist with Agnostic
An Atheist asserts that God does NOT exist, whereas an Agnostic believes human knowledge cannot prove whether God exists or not.
🚫
Mistake 2: Mixing Up Egoist and Egotist
An Egoist is selfish and self-centered; an Egotist is an arrogant talker who constantly boasts about himself.
🚫
Mistake 3: Confusing Biography with Autobiography
A Biography is a life story written by someone else; an Autobiography is written by the person himself.
