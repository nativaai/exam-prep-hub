The 6-Pillar Preparation Framework

Rather than memorizing isolated daily news, competitive exams categorize Current Affairs into structured, recurring institutional pillars over the last 6 to 12 months.

Core Focus Pillars:
• Government Schemes: Nodal ministry, budgetary outlay, target demographic, launch year.
• International Summits: Host country, theme, member additions (e.g. G20, BRICS, SCO).
• Defence & Space: Bilateral military drills, ISRO satellite launches, defence pacts.
• Indices & Reports: Publishing agency, India's rank, top-ranking nation.
📐

High-Yield Categorical Breakdown

🏛️ Major International Groups
G20 | BRICS | SCO | ASEAN
Track Presidency rotation, headquarters (SCO in Beijing, ASEAN in Jakarta), and new member inductions.
🛡️ Key Bilateral Defence Drills
Yudh Abhyas | Surya Kiran | Mitra Shakti
Yudh Abhyas (India-USA), Surya Kiran (India-Nepal), Mitra Shakti (India-Sri Lanka), Sampriti (India-Bangladesh).
🚀 Space & Technology Missions
Gaganyaan | Aditya-L1 | Chandrayaan
Launch vehicle specifications (PSLV vs LVM3), payloads, orbits (Lagrange Point L1), and scientific objectives.
📊 Global Reports & Indices
HDI | Global Innovation Index
HDI published by UNDP; World Press Freedom by RSF; Ease of Doing Business (archived); Global Hunger Index.

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Which organization publishes the annual Human Development Index (HDI) report?
Options: (A) World Bank  |  (B) International Monetary Fund (IMF)  |  (C) United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)  |  (D) World Economic Forum (WEF)
Index Structure HDI measures life expectancy, education (mean & expected years of schooling), and Gross National Income per capita.
Publishing Agency HDI was created by Pakistani economist Mahbub ul Haq and is published by UNDP.
Solution Correct option is UNDP (Option C).
✓ Final Answer: UNDP
Ex 2 Match the bilateral military/naval exercise with the partner nation:
1. JIMEX  |  2. Garuda Shakti  |  3. Nomadic Elephant  |  4. Dharma Guardian
Exercise Identification • JIMEX & Dharma Guardian: India & Japan.
• Garuda Shakti: India & Indonesia.
• Nomadic Elephant: India & Mongolia.
✓ Bilateral Defence Exercises Verified

Speed-Solving Tips & Retention Shortcuts

📅
Timeline Rule (Last 6 Months): 70-80% of current affairs questions come from the 6 months immediately preceding the exam date.
🏢
International Headquarter Clusters:
• Geneva: WHO, WTO, ILO, WIPO, UNHCR.
• Vienna: IAEA, OPEC, UNIDO.
• Washington D.C.: World Bank, IMF.
• Paris: UNESCO, OECD, FATF.
🏆
Major Award Categories: Track recipients of Nobel Prizes, Bharat Ratna, Jnanpith Award, Dadasaheb Phalke Award, Ramon Magsaysay, and Booker Prize.
🎯
Scheme Deconstruction Rule: For every new welfare initiative, note 4 points: Launch Year, Ministry, Target Beneficiaries, Financial Limit/Insurance Cover.
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing Exercise "Garuda" with "Garuda Shakti"
Garuda is an air exercise between India & France. Garuda Shakti is a joint military drill between India & Indonesia. Shakti alone is with France. Samudra Shakti is naval with Indonesia.
🚫
Mistake 2: Reading Daily News Without Note Consolidation
Reading news without categorizing into fixed monthly tables (Summits, Appointments, Books, Exercises) leads to total recall failure during the examination.
🚫
Mistake 3: Overlooking Nodal Ministries for Schemes
Questions often ask "Which Ministry implements PM-KISAN or PM-Awas Yojana?" rather than just the scheme benefits. Always link the scheme to its ministry.
