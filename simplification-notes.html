Order of Operations: VBODMAS

To simplify complex mathematical expressions without ambiguity, mathematical operations must be evaluated strictly following the VBODMAS hierarchy from left to right.

Hierarchy Breakdown:
• V = Vinculum / Bar  a − b
• B = Brackets  ( ) → { } → [ ]
• O = 'Of' (Multiplication prioritized over ÷)
• D & M = Division & Multiplication (Left to Right)
• A & S = Addition & Subtraction (Left to Right)
1. V / BBar & Brackets
2. OOf (Power / Root)
3. D / MDivision & Multi
4. A / SAdd & Subtract
Evaluation Priority (Top → Bottom)

Core Identities & Indices Rules

🔹 Difference of Squares & Cubes
a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)
a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)
⚡ Laws of Indices
aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ | (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
aᵐ / aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ  |  a⁰ = 1
🎯 Sum & Difference Expansion
(a + b)² + (a − b)² = 2(a² + b²)
(a + b)² − (a − b)² = 4ab
📐 Conditional Identity
If a + b + c = 0 → a³ + b³ + c³ = 3abc
Saves long calculations in 3-variable cubes.

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Evaluate: 25 − [16 + {12 − (9 − 3 + 2)}]
Vinculum/Innermost (3 + 2 with bar) = 5 → Round Bracket: (9 − 5) = 4
Curly Bracket {12 − 4} = 8
Square Bracket & Outer [16 + 8] = 24 → 25 − 24 = 1
✓ Final Answer: 1
Ex 2 Evaluate: 36 ÷ 6 of 3 × 2 + (8 − 3)
Priority 1 ('Of') 6 of 3 = 18 (computed before division!)
Priority 2 (÷ & ×) 36 ÷ 18 = 2 → 2 × 2 = 4
Priority 3 (+ & Brackets) (8 − 3) = 5 → 4 + 5 = 9
✓ Final Answer: 9
Ex 3 Simplify: (0.73 × 0.73 × 0.73 + 0.27 × 0.27 × 0.27) / (0.73² − 0.73 × 0.27 + 0.27²)
Pattern Form: (a³ + b³) / (a² − ab + b²) where a = 0.73, b = 0.27
Identity a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²) → Expression simplifies to (a + b)
Solution 0.73 + 0.27 = 1.00
✓ Final Answer: 1

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

✨
Digital Root Trick: Add digits of numbers until single digits remain; equate LHS and RHS digital roots to eliminate wrong options instantly.
⚡
Unit Digit Verification: For large addition/multiplication options with distinct last digits, calculate only the unit digits.
🎯
Fraction Addition Shortcut: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd. For mixed fractions: add wholes and fractions separately.
🔍
Surds Rationalization: 1 / (√a − √b) = (√a + √b) / (a − b).
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Treating 'Of' as Normal Multiplication in Order
'Of' must always be solved before Division. E.g., 20 ÷ 5 of 2 = 20 ÷ 10 = 2, NOT (20 ÷ 5) × 2 = 8.
🚫
Mistake 2: Sign Errors During Bracket Expansion
When a minus sign precedes parentheses, every term inside changes sign upon expansion: − (a − b + c) = −a + b − c.
🚫
Mistake 3: Misapplying Division from Left to Right
Chained divisions must be calculated strictly from left to right: a ÷ b ÷ c = (a / b) / c = a / (b × c).
