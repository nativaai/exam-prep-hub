Standard Family Tree Symbols

Blood relation questions test logical deduction of kinship across generations. Mapping with standard tree notations eliminates confusion.

Standard Symbol Legend:
• Male (+ / [ ])  |  Female (− / ( ))
• Married Couple: Double horizontal line A ═ B
• Siblings: Single horizontal line A ─ B
• Generations: Vertical line (Parent above, Child below)
📐

Key Kinship Terminology

👨 Paternal Relations (Father's Side)
Uncle / Aunt / Cousin
Father's Brother = Paternal Uncle; Father's Sister = Paternal Aunt.
👩 Maternal Relations (Mother's Side)
Uncle / Aunt / Cousin
Mother's Brother = Maternal Uncle; Mother's Sister = Maternal Aunt.
💍 In-Law Relations (Spouse Side)
Brother/Sister/Father-in-law
Spouse's Brother = Brother-in-law; Sister's Husband = Brother-in-law.
👶 Nephew & Niece
Sibling's Son / Daughter
Brother/Sister's Son = Nephew; Brother/Sister's Daughter = Niece.

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of the only son of my grandfather." How is the woman in the photograph related to the man?
Breakdown "My grandfather's only son" = Speaker's Father
Kinship Link "Daughter of my father" = Speaker's Sister
Solution The woman in the photograph is the speaker's Sister.
✓ Final Answer: Sister
Ex 2 A is B's brother. C is A's mother. D is C's father. E is B's son. How is D related to E?
Tree Relations (D+) → Father of (C−) → Mother of (A+) and (B) → Parent of (E+)
Generations Generation 1: D | Generation 2: C | Generation 3: B | Generation 4: E (3 generations gap)
Solution D is the father of E's grandmother (C) → D is the Great-Grandfather of E.
✓ Final Answer: Great-Grandfather
Ex 3 If 'P + Q' means P is mother of Q; 'P − Q' means P is brother of Q; 'P × Q' means P is father of Q. In the expression A × B + C − D, how is A related to D?
Decoding A × B → A is father of B (+) | B + C → B is mother of C (−) | C − D → C is brother of D (+)
Deduction B is mother of D. A is father of B.
Solution A is the father of D's mother → Maternal Grandfather.
✓ Final Answer: Maternal Grandfather

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

🧩
Gender Elimination: In coded blood relations, determine the required gender of the target person first to eliminate options without drawing the full tree.
⚡
Generation Gap Check: Assign values (+1 for parent, 0 for sibling/spouse, −1 for child). Total generation score filters out invalid answers immediately.
🔄
Backtracking in Pointing Questions: Break long dialogue sentences from the end ("my father's mother's only son...") back to the start.
🎯
"Only Child" Clue: "Only son of my father" = Speaker himself (if male); "Only daughter of my mother" = Speaker herself (if female).
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Assuming Gender from Names
Never assume gender based solely on a person's name (e.g., 'Kiran' or 'Deepak') unless explicitly mentioned in the question prompt.
🚫
Mistake 2: Reversing the Target Relationship
Pay close attention to who is asked: "How is A related to B?" means "What is A to B?" (e.g., Uncle, not Nephew).
🚫
Mistake 3: Confusing Paternal and Maternal Lines
Be vigilant when options distinguish between Maternal Uncle (Mama) vs Paternal Uncle (Chacha/Tau).
