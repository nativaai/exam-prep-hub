Pattern Identification Framework

A Series is a sequential arrangement of numbers, letters, or words arranged according to a distinct mathematical or logical rule.

Step-by-Step Diagnostic Hierarchy:
• Gradual Growth: Addition / Subtraction (Difference Table)
• Steep Growth: Multiplication, Cubes, or Factorials
• Oscillating/Zig-Zag: Alternating Dual Series (Skip 1 term)
📐

Core Series Pattern Archetypes

🔹 Differences of Differences
Δ₁ → Δ₂ = Constant / AP
When first differences aren't equal, take differences of differences.
📈 Squares & Cubes (n² ± k / n³ ± k)
n² ± 1, n³ ± 1, n³ − n
e.g., 0, 6, 24, 60, 120, 210 follows n³ − n.
⚡ Mixed / Triangular Operations
× k + c  |  × 1 + 1, × 2 + 2...
Multiplication combined with progressive integer additions.
🔀 Alternating Dual Series
Odd Pos. vs Even Pos.
Two independent series interleaved into a single sequence.

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Find the missing term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, 42, ?
Given 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, 42, ?
Differences 6−2 = 4, 12−6 = 6, 20−12 = 8, 30−20 = 10, 42−30 = 12
Solution Next difference must be +14 → 42 + 14 = 56 (or n² + n → 7² + 7 = 56)

✓ Final Answer: 56
Ex 2 Find the next number in the sequence: 3, 7, 16, 35, 74, ?
Multiplication Pattern 3 × 2 + 1 = 7  |  7 × 2 + 2 = 16  |  16 × 2 + 3 = 35  |  35 × 2 + 4 = 74
Rule Multiply each term by 2 and add progressive integers (+1, +2, +3, +4, +5)
Solution 74 × 2 + 5 = 148 + 5 = 153
✓ Final Answer: 153
Ex 3 Find the wrong number in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, 34, 48, 63
Square Analysis (n² − 1) 2²−1=3, 3²−1=8, 4²−1=15, 5²−1=24, 6²−1=35 (given 34), 7²−1=48, 8²−1=63
Discrepancy 34 does not match the (n² − 1) series rule; it should be 35.
Solution The wrong term in the series is 34.
✓ Final Answer: 34 (Correct is 35)

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

🌳
Difference Tree: If no immediate pattern appears, write down the 1st differences and 2nd differences; 90% of exam series resolve by the 2nd layer.
⚡
Half-Multipliers (.5 Series): If the 2nd term drops or stays identical then climbs rapidly (e.g., 8, 4, 4, 6, 12), test ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5.
🔀
Long Series Clue: Whenever a series has 8 or more terms given, it is almost certainly a dual or triple interleaved series.
🎯
Fibonacci Variations: Check if each term is the sum of previous two terms: T_n = T_{n-1} + T_{n-2}.
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Validating on Too Few Terms
Always verify your identified pattern on at least 3 consecutive transitions before calculating the missing number.
🚫
Mistake 2: Missing Decimal/Fractional Multipliers
Do not restrict checks to integer multipliers. Patterns like ×0.5, ×1.5, ×2.5 are frequent in modern exams.
🚫
Mistake 3: Identifying the Replacement Instead of the Wrong Term
In "Find the Wrong Term" questions, select the faulty number present in the series, not what it should become.
