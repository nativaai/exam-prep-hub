🏷️ Profit & Loss - Study Notes

Master Cost Price (CP), Selling Price (SP), Marked Price (MP), successive discounts, and dishonest dealer problems.

₹
💡

Concept & Definition

Cost Price (CP): The price at which an article is purchased, including overhead expenses (transportation, repairs, packaging).

Selling Price (SP): The price at which an article is sold to a customer.

Core Conditions:
• If SP > CP → Gain/Profit = SP − CP
• If CP > SP → Loss = CP − SP
• Profit% or Loss% is always calculated on Cost Price (CP) unless explicitly stated.
📐

Core Profit & Loss Formulas

📈 Profit Percentage
Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
SP = CP × [(100 + P%) / 100]
📉 Loss Percentage
Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100
SP = CP × [(100 − L%) / 100]
🏷️ Discount & Marked Price
Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100
Discount = MP − SP (calculated on MP).
⚡ Successive Discounts
Single Equiv % = d₁ + d₂ − (d₁d₂ / 100)
Single discount equivalent to discounts d₁% and d₂%.
📝

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 A trader buys an article for ₹800 and sells it for ₹1,000. Find his profit percentage.
Given Cost Price (CP) = ₹800, Selling Price (SP) = ₹1,000
Formula Profit = SP − CP → Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100
Solution Profit = 1000 − 800 = ₹200 → Profit % = (200 / 800) × 100 = 25%
✓ Final Answer: 25% Profit
Ex 2 An article marked at ₹1,500 is sold after two successive discounts of 20% and 10%. Find the final Selling Price.
Given Marked Price (MP) = ₹1,500, d₁ = 20%, d₂ = 10%
Formula SP = MP × [(100 − d₁) / 100] × [(100 − d₂) / 100]
Solution SP = 1500 × (80 / 100) × (90 / 100) = 1500 × 0.8 × 0.9 = ₹1,080
Marked Price ₹1,500
→ -20% →
Intermediate ₹1,200
→ -10% →
Final SP ₹1,080
✓ Final Answer: SP = ₹1,080
Ex 3 Two articles are sold for ₹990 each. On one, the seller gains 10%, and on the other, he loses 10%. Find the overall gain or loss percentage.
Given Same SP = ₹990, Equal Gain & Loss % (x = 10%)
Shortcut Rule When two items are sold at same SP, with x% gain and x% loss → Always a Loss = (x / 10)² %
Solution Loss % = (10 / 10)² = (1)² = 1% Loss
✓ Final Answer: 1% Overall Loss
⭐

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

⚖️
Dishonest Dealer Formula: Gain % = [Error / (True Value − Error)] × 100. (e.g., using 900g instead of 1000g → [100 / 900] × 100 = 11.11% profit).
⚡
Goods Sold at CP using False Weight: Gain % = [(True Weight − False Weight) / False Weight] × 100.
🎯
Relation between CP, MP, and Discount: CP / MP = (100 − Discount %) / (100 + Profit %).
✨
Cost Price of x = Selling Price of y: Profit % = [(x − y) / y] × 100. If negative, it represents a Loss.
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Calculating Discount on Cost Price
Discount is always calculated on the Marked Price (MP), while Profit and Loss are calculated on the Cost Price (CP).
🚫
Mistake 2: Assuming No Profit No Loss for Equal Gain/Loss on SP
When two items are sold at the same SP with x% profit and x% loss, there is always an overall loss of (x / 10)² %. (It is 0% only when the CP is the same).
🚫
Mistake 3: Forgetting Overhead Charges
Repairs, transportation, and setup costs must always be added to the initial purchase price to form the Total Real Cost Price (Effective CP).
