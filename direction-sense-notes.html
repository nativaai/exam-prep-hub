Cardinal & Intercardinal Directions

Direction problems track movement across 4 primary (Cardinal) and 4 secondary (Intercardinal / Ordinal) vectors on a 2D plane.

Turn & Angle Rules:
• Right Turn: Clockwise rotation by 90°
• Left Turn: Anti-clockwise rotation by 90°
• Intercardinal directions (NE, NW, SE, SW) lie at exactly 45° between adjacent cardinal axes.
📐

Core Displacement & Shadow Rules

📏 Shortest Distance (Pythagoras)
d = √(Δx² + Δy²)
Displacement between starting and ending coordinates.
🌅 Morning Shadow (Sunrise in East)
Shadow Falls to the WEST
If facing North → Shadow is to the Left. If facing South → Shadow is to the Right.
🌇 Evening Shadow (Sunset in West)
Shadow Falls to the EAST
If facing North → Shadow is to the Right. If facing South → Shadow is to the Left.
🔄 Net Rotation Angle
Net = Σ Clockwise − Σ Anti-Clockwise
Calculate the net degree shift from the initial facing direction.
📝

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Rohan walks 8 km North, turns Right and walks 6 km. Find his shortest distance and direction from the starting point.
Path North = 8 km (y-axis), East = 6 km (x-axis)
Pythagoras Distance = √(8² + 6²) = √(64 + 36) = √100 = 10 km
Direction Relative to origin (0,0), point (6,8) is in the North-East quadrant.
✓ Final Answer: 10 km, North-East
Ex 2 A person facing North turns 135° in the clockwise direction and then 180° in the anti-clockwise direction. Which direction is he facing now?
Initial Facing North (0°)
Net Rotation Net Angle = 135° (CW) − 180° (ACW) = −45° (i.e. 45° Anti-Clockwise)
Solution 45° Anti-Clockwise from North = North-West (NW)
✓ Final Answer: North-West
Ex 3 One morning after sunrise, Suresh was standing facing a pole. The shadow of the pole fell exactly to his right. Which direction was Suresh facing?
Condition Time: Morning (Sun in East → Shadow falls towards West)
Deduction Since shadow is on Suresh's Right side, his Right side points West
Solution If West is to the Right, Suresh must be facing South.
✓ Final Answer: South

Speed-Solving Tips & Shortcuts

✨
Right/Left Cancellation: 1 Right turn (+90°) cancels 1 Left turn (−90°). If a question only asks for the final facing direction, cancel out equal R and L turns.
☀️
Noon Shadow Rule: At exactly 12:00 PM (solar noon), the sun is directly overhead and casts no directional shadow.
📐
Coordinate System: Treat East as +x, West as −x, North as +y, South as −y. Add all vectors to find final coordinates without drawing.
🎯
Consecutive 4 Turns: 4 consecutive Left or Right turns bring you back to the exact starting facing direction (360° rotation).
⚠️

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing Total Distance with Shortest Distance
"Total distance traveled" is the arithmetic sum of all path segments (8 + 6 = 14 km), whereas "distance from starting point" requires displacement (√(8² + 6²) = 10 km).
🚫
Mistake 2: Misinterpreting the Reference Origin Point
Always check whether the question asks "Direction of End point from Starting point" (NE) or "Direction of Start point from End point" (SW).
🚫
Mistake 3: Reversing Left and Right When Facing South/West
When facing South, turning Right moves towards West, and turning Left moves towards East. Never use your screen orientation directly without rotating your mental frame.
