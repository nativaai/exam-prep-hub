Chronological Era Periodization

Indian History is divided into three major eras for competitive examinations: Ancient, Medieval, and Modern.

Era Breakdown:
• Ancient India (2500 BCE - 750 CE): Indus Valley Civilization → Vedic Period → Mauryas & Guptas.
• Medieval India (750 CE - 1707 CE): Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526) → Vijayanagara & Empire → Mughal Empire.
• Modern India (1707 CE - 1947 CE): British Expansion → Revolt of 1857 → INC & Freedom Struggle.

Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Ex 1 Arrange the following events in chronological order:
1. Partition of Bengal  |  2. Quit India Movement  |  3. Non-Cooperation Movement  |  4. Jallianwala Bagh Massacre
Year Analysis • Partition of Bengal = 1905 (Lord Curzon)
• Jallianwala Bagh = 1919 (13th April)
• Non-Cooperation Movement = 1920 (Mahatma Gandhi)
• Quit India Movement = 1942 (8th August)
Order Chronological order is 1 → 4 → 3 → 2.
✓ Final Answer: 1 - 4 - 3 - 2
Ex 2 Match the famous battles with their respective years and key leaders:
A. First Battle of Panipat  |  B. Battle of Plassey  |  C. Battle of Buxar
Battle Facts • First Battle of Panipat (1526): Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi (Established Mughal Empire).
• Battle of Plassey (1757): Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah.
• Battle of Buxar (1764): Hector Munro defeated joint forces of Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daula, and Shah Alam II.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

🚫
Mistake 1: Confusing Governor-General of Bengal vs India vs Viceroy
Warren Hastings: 1st Gov-Gen of Bengal (1773). Lord William Bentinck: 1st Gov-Gen of India (Charter Act 1833). Lord Canning: 1st Viceroy of India (Govt of India Act 1858).
🚫
Mistake 2: Mixing Up the Battle of Plassey and Battle of Buxar
Plassey (1757) laid the initial political foothold for the EIC in Bengal; Buxar (1764) firmly established real British administrative supremacy over Mughal territories.
🚫
Mistake 3: Reversing Buddhist Councils Locations and Patrons
1st Council: Rajgriha (Ajatashatru) | 2nd: Vaishali (Kalasoka) | 3rd: Pataliputra (Ashoka) | 4th: Kundalvana, Kashmir (Kanishka).
